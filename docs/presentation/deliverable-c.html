C Deliverable C · Differentiation snapshot

Everyone will show the texted link. We built what sits above it.

The sponsor FAQ told every team to design for a vendor who never logs in — SMS / magic-link. That's the floor, and we built it. We just don't claim it as our idea. The differentiation is the seven things we stacked on top.

Above the line — what we built on top of the baseline

Silence ladder

Unanswered at 2h the system nags; 2h later it escalates. Non-response becomes a timestamped event with an owner.

Verified vs vendor-reported

Every status badged by evidence. An unproven "delivered" opens its own escalation; a driver POD opens nothing.

Nurse-primary pickup

The nurse in the room is the trigger, the EMR the redundant fallback — the direction the baseline link never reaches.

Risk before the deadline

Rules-based, in sentences a case manager can argue with, with a one-click vendor swap.

Two trust levels

A tap needs no model; free text is confidence-gated to a human queue. Three personas, one ledger.

The CEO named this one

The household is the only witness — so ask it

A condition survey isn't new; who owns the answers is. Today it lives in the file of the supplier being graded. We invert the direction: the hospice asks, holds the record, and it lands on the scorecard that picks the next vendor.
