Where the data comes from — the one line that has to be right

The equipment catalog and prices are real CMS data — the Medicare DMEPOS Public Use File: HCPCS codes, descriptions, national beneficiary counts, average allowed amounts. Everything else — 12 patients, 3 vendor personalities, a simulated year of orders, every delivery time and outcome — is synthetic and labeled synthetic, because CMS publishes billing, not logistics. Both phone screens are simulators; there is no SMS gateway, and we say so before anyone asks. The magic links in the bubbles are real /portal/<token> URLs.
